# BUSINESS_PRIORITIES_AND_AUDIENCES.md — Times Estate Ltd

## Status of this document

**Approved by the owner (2026-09-07) as a working baseline for Day 3
onward — still changeable at any time**, not a permanently fixed
decision. The ranking and audience list below were proposed by Times
Estate Ltd's website team based on how estate agencies of this type
typically generate revenue and retain clients; the owner has agreed to
proceed on this basis while reserving the right to amend it later,
particularly once still-Missing information (contact details, fees,
confirmed geographic coverage) is supplied.

No audience or service outside the twelve confirmed service areas has been
included.

## 1. Proposed business priorities (ranked)

| Rank | Priority | Why this ranking is proposed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate qualified sales and lettings valuation requests (residential and commercial) | This is the primary new-business entry point for an estate agency; every site reviewed in `MARKET_RESEARCH.md` treats it as the top call-to-action |
| 2 | Support and retain existing managed landlords and tenants | Property management is recurring revenue; a poor repair-reporting or communication experience risks losing management instructions, which are harder to replace than a single sale |
| 3 | Convert commercial sales, lettings and management enquiries | Confirmed as a core service line, not a sideline — needs its own well-built journey rather than a residential template with different labels |
| 4 | Convert buyer and tenant property search into viewing requests | Necessary to fill the pipeline the valuations above create, but secondary to winning the instruction in the first place |
| 5 | Demonstrate genuine regulatory trust and compliance | Supports every other priority rather than standing alone — covered by Client Money Protection, redress scheme and other confirmed credentials once verified |
| 6 | Reduce administrative overhead through lead-routing and controlled AI assistance | An efficiency and consistency goal for the business once the above are working, not a reason to launch with unfinished core journeys |

## 2. Audience profiles

Each profile below corresponds to a genuine consumer of one or more of the
twelve confirmed service areas. None of these are new services — they are
different people encountering the same confirmed services from different
angles.

### Residential vendors
- **Main objective:** Sell their home for the best achievable price with
  minimal disruption.
- **Principal concerns:** Accurate valuation, marketing quality, agent
  responsiveness, fees, chain management.
- **Information required:** How valuations work, fee structure (once
  supplied — currently Missing), marketing approach, recent track record
  (only if genuinely evidenced).
- **Preferred action:** Book a free valuation.
- **Trust requirements:** Regulatory credentials, clear fee transparency,
  genuine local knowledge.
- **Likely barriers:** Scepticism about inflated valuations, concern about
  being locked into a long tie-in agreement.
- **Recommended website journey:** Homepage or "Sell" landing page →
  valuation request form → confirmation and expected response time.

### Residential landlords
- **Main objective:** Let their property quickly to a reliable tenant at
  the best achievable rent, with compliant paperwork.
- **Principal concerns:** Void periods, tenant vetting, legal compliance
  (deposit protection, right-to-rent, safety certificates), whether to
  self-manage or use full management.
- **Information required:** Letting vs. full management options and fees
  (Missing), compliance responsibilities, CMP/redress protection.
- **Preferred action:** Book a rental valuation / request a lettings
  appraisal.
- **Trust requirements:** CMP and redress scheme membership clearly
  displayed (once Confirmed), clear explanation of what management
  includes.
- **Likely barriers:** Fee transparency concerns, uncertainty about
  legal obligations.
- **Recommended website journey:** "Let" landing page → rental valuation
  request → follow-up with letting vs. management options.

### Buyers
- **Main objective:** Find a suitable property and secure it.
- **Principal concerns:** Accurate, current listings; ability to arrange
  viewings quickly; understanding local areas.
- **Information required:** Search by location/price/type, property detail
  pages, area information.
- **Preferred action:** Register for property alerts / book a viewing.
- **Trust requirements:** Listings must be genuinely current — a stale or
  demo listing shown as if live would breach Rule 1 and damage trust
  immediately.
- **Likely barriers:** Frustration with irrelevant search results, unclear
  next steps after finding a property.
- **Recommended website journey:** Property search → property detail page
  → viewing request → confirmation.

### Tenants
- **Main objective:** Find a suitable rental property and move in with
  minimal friction.
- **Principal concerns:** Availability dates, deposit and referencing
  process, whether pets/guarantors are accepted.
- **Information required:** Same as buyers, plus lettings-specific detail
  (deposit amount, min tenancy length, referencing process).
- **Preferred action:** Book a viewing / register for alerts.
- **Trust requirements:** Clear, accurate availability; no listings shown
  after they are let.
- **Likely barriers:** Competitive rental market anxiety, unclear
  referencing requirements.
- **Recommended website journey:** Property search (to rent) → property
  detail page → viewing request → confirmation.

### Existing managed tenants
- **Main objective:** Get a repair fixed or a query answered quickly,
  without having to phone during office hours.
- **Principal concerns:** How to report a repair, expected response time,
  emergency contact process out of hours.
- **Information required:** A simple repair-reporting method and what
  happens next.
- **Preferred action:** Report a repair.
- **Trust requirements:** Confidence the report has actually been
  received and will be actioned.
- **Likely barriers:** Not knowing this option exists on the website at
  all if it is buried in a portal login.
- **Recommended website journey:** Prominent "Report a repair" link
  (not buried) → simple form with property/issue detail → confirmation
  with reference number → internal notification to the management team.

### Commercial landlords
- **Main objective:** Let or manage commercial premises to a suitable
  occupier with minimal void periods.
- **Principal concerns:** Tenant covenant strength, lease terms, service
  charge and management arrangements.
- **Information required:** Commercial lettings/management service
  detail, sector coverage (once confirmed).
- **Preferred action:** Request a commercial lettings/management
  appraisal.
- **Trust requirements:** Evidence of genuine commercial expertise
  distinct from residential.
- **Likely barriers:** Assuming a primarily residential-looking agency
  lacks serious commercial capability.
- **Recommended website journey:** Commercial landing page → sector-
  specific detail → enquiry form → follow-up call.

### Commercial occupiers
- **Main objective:** Find suitable premises for their business (office,
  retail, industrial, etc. — exact sectors to be confirmed).
- **Principal concerns:** Location, size, lease length/flexibility, cost.
- **Information required:** Commercial property search/listing detail.
- **Preferred action:** Enquire about a specific property or a general
  requirement.
- **Trust requirements:** Accurate, current availability.
- **Likely barriers:** Commercial searches are often more bespoke than
  residential — a generic residential-style search may frustrate this
  audience.
- **Recommended website journey:** Commercial search/listing → enquiry
  form with requirement detail → follow-up.

### Property investors
- **Main objective:** Identify property (residential or commercial) that
  meets an investment return requirement, and arrange ongoing management.
- **Principal concerns:** Yield, area growth potential, hands-off
  management options.
- **Information required:** Management service detail, investment-relevant
  property information (only where genuinely available — no invented
  yield figures or projections).
- **Preferred action:** Enquire about management services or a specific
  investment property.
- **Trust requirements:** No speculative or invented return figures —
  Rule 1 applies strictly here, as investment claims carry particular
  regulatory sensitivity.
- **Likely barriers:** Distrust of unsubstantiated investment claims.
- **Recommended website journey:** Property search or management landing
  page → enquiry form → follow-up conversation (any yield/return
  discussion handled personally, not as a published website claim).

### Overseas landlords
- **Main objective:** Manage a UK property remotely with confidence it is
  being looked after properly.
- **Principal concerns:** Communication method/frequency, non-resident
  landlord tax scheme awareness, time-zone-friendly contact.
- **Information required:** Full management service detail, how reporting
  and communication work at a distance.
- **Preferred action:** Request a management appraisal.
- **Trust requirements:** Clarity that they will not need to be physically
  present; clear points of contact.
- **Likely barriers:** Trusting an agent they cannot easily visit in
  person; concern about being a lower priority than local landlords.
- **Recommended website journey:** Management landing page (clearly
  covering remote/overseas landlords) → appraisal request → confirmation
  of communication approach.

### Developers
- **Main objective:** Sell or let a completed development, or appoint an
  agent for an upcoming scheme.
- **Principal concerns:** Track record with developments of a similar
  scale, marketing reach, sales/lettings speed.
- **Information required:** New-homes/development service detail (only if
  Times Estate Ltd genuinely offers this — to be confirmed; not assumed).
- **Preferred action:** Direct enquiry, not a generic web form.
- **Trust requirements:** Genuine evidence of relevant experience —
  no invented case studies.
- **Likely barriers:** Developers typically engage agents through direct
  relationships rather than a website form — this journey should be
  simple and low-friction rather than heavily built out.
- **Recommended website journey:** Short "Developments" or "New Homes"
  page (only if confirmed as a service) → direct contact details → no
  unnecessary multi-step form.

*Note: "Developers" as a named audience was included here because it is a
natural sub-audience of confirmed sales/commercial services, but Times
Estate Ltd has not explicitly confirmed development/new-homes work as a
distinct offering. This page should only be built if the owner confirms it
applies — otherwise this audience is served adequately by the general
Commercial Sales section.*

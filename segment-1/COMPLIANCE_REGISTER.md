# COMPLIANCE_REGISTER.md — Times Estate Ltd (Segment 1, 2026-10-19)

**This register does not claim that Times Estate Ltd or either website is
legally compliant.** It records, for each legal topic in the brief: the
official source, the date it was last checked, whether it applies, the
evidence required, the current position found on the live site and in
the local build, the website action, the responsible person, and a
status of **VERIFIED / MISSING / NOT APPLICABLE / PROFESSIONAL REVIEW
REQUIRED**. "Owner" = Mohammad Muzaffar Malik (director). "Solicitor" =
a UK solicitor or compliance adviser the owner appoints. Every item
marked PROFESSIONAL REVIEW REQUIRED stays open until a named
professional signs it off; a row with status VERIFIED means the fact
was checked against the source named, not that the whole obligation is
discharged.

Sources checked on 2026-10-19 unless stated: legislation.gov.uk,
Companies House, Propertymark, Shelter Legal, Gowling WLG and Kennedys
commentaries (secondary sources are marked *secondary*). Where the
official text could not be fetched in this environment, the row says so.

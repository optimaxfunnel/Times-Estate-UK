# Sample listing photos — licence record

Downloaded 24 November 2026 from **Unsplash**, at the owner's request
("get images from free sources … where properties images are missing").
None was taken from another estate agent's or property portal's website.

**These are stock interiors, not Times Estate Ltd properties.** They are
shown only on the fabricated sample listings (CLAUDE.md Rules 1–2). Every
card still says "Sample property — not real", and each property page says
"Sample photo — stock photographs for a demonstration listing, not this
property". Interiors were chosen deliberately: a photo of a real house
front could make a real home look as if it were for sale with Times
Estate Ltd.

When real listings replace the samples, these photos stop appearing
automatically (they are only used for records marked as demonstration).

## Licence

All seven are under the **Unsplash Licence** (https://unsplash.com/license):
free to use, including commercially, without permission. Credit is not
required, but the site credits each photographer on the property page
anyway. Each photo was checked as a free photo, not an "Unsplash+" one,
on 24 November 2026. The licence does not allow selling the photos
unaltered or using them to build a competing photo service; neither
applies here.

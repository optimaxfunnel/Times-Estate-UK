## What the licences require, and how the site meets it

- **Credit (all five):** each slide shows "Photo: [photographer], [licence]",
  linking to the source page and the licence.
- **Share-alike (the four CC BY-SA photos):** if a photo is *adapted*,
  the adapted version must carry the same licence. The website versions
  are only cropped to 16:9 and resized; the darkening behind the text is
  a separate layer on the page, not a change to the image files. If an
  edited version is ever made (for example a retouched or combined
  image), publish it under the same licence and say it was changed.
- **No endorsement:** nothing on the site may suggest the photographers
  endorse Times Estate Ltd.
